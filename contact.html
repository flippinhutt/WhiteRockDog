Questions we hear

Download your White Rock Dog Rescue welcome packet

How do I adopt?

Browse our dogs on the adopt page and fill out the adoption form.

What does a foster need to provide?

A safe, loving home and rides to required vetting appointments. We provide all necessary supplies, including food and a crate.

Can I adopt my foster dog?

Yes. Fosters get first right of refusal on adopting their foster dog.

Where does my donation go?

Directly to food and low-cost vet care. We are 100% volunteer-based, and donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.
